    Three generations at the copper kettle

    Marshall's legendary Mackinac Island fudge is 3rd generation family owned and operated. Following recipes passed down over our 100 year history, skilled chocolatier and candy makers combine the finest ingredients, with no preservatives, to create a tasteable difference.

    1. 1923Jim Marshall starts as a candy maker at Mackinac Island's very first candy shop
    2. 1952Jim, Oradelle and son Dean open Marshall's Driftwood Fudge on Main Street, Mackinac Island
    3. 1965store opens on East Central Avenue, Mackinaw City, where it still is today
    4. Todayowned and run by Daisy (Jim's granddaughter) and her husband Pat; fudge cooked in copper kettles and finished on marble tables
